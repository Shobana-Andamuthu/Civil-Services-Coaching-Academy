About Civil Services Academy

A scholarly academy dedicated to preparing aspirants for the UPSC Civil Services Examination.

Our Story

Built around the aspirant's journey

Civil Services Academy was created to give UPSC aspirants a focused, disciplined and supportive place to prepare. We combine subject expertise with structured practice so that preparation stays clear, steady and purposeful.

Our programs follow the examination's stages, and every aspirant is guided by mentors who monitor progress and help adjust the plan.

What Guides Us

Mission, vision and values

Our Mission

To prepare capable, ethical and well-informed aspirants through rigorous teaching and honest, regular feedback.

Our Vision

To be a trusted academy where aspirants grow into public servants guided by integrity and service.

Our Values

Discipline, intellectual honesty, mentorship and respect for every aspirant's pace and background.

Our Journey

Milestones along the way

How the academy has grown alongside its aspirants.

  1. 2016

    Academy founded

    Started with a single Foundation batch and a small team of subject experts.

  2. 2018

    Mains answer-writing program

    Introduced daily answer practice with structured faculty evaluation.

  3. 2020

    Online dashboard launched

    Aspirants gained anytime access to materials, tests and schedules.

  4. 2023

    Interview guidance panel

    Mock Personality Tests with experienced mentors and DAF reviews.

  5. 2025

    Expanded mentoring

    Personal mentors now follow every aspirant from Prelims to Interview.
