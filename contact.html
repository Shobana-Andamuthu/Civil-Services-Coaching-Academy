Questions

Frequently asked questions

How soon will the academy reply to my enquiry?

Our counsellors usually respond within one working day by phone or email.

Can I visit the campus before enrolling?

Yes. Walk in between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, to meet a counsellor and see the reading room.

Is a demo class available?

A demo class is available for the Foundation Program. Ask a counsellor for the next session.

Are online or weekend classes offered?

Yes. The Weekend Batch is built for working professionals, and recordings are available on your dashboard.

How do I get fee and instalment details?

Share your chosen program through the enquiry form or call us, and our team will send the fee structure and instalment options.
