Frequently asked questions

Who can join the Foundation Program?

Graduates and final-year students planning to appear for the Civil Services Examination can enrol. Contact us to confirm the current batch details.

Are tests and materials available online?

Yes. Enrolled aspirants use the dashboard to access study materials, review test performance and see class and examination schedules.

Is personal mentoring included?

Each program includes mentor guidance. The exact format is shared by our counsellors when you enquire.

How do I enrol?

Use the Enroll Now button to reach our contact page and share your details with the academy team.
